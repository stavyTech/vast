Our Story

Born From a Bold Vision

Nkiri Synergies Ltd was founded with a clear belief: that Africa's greatest resource is its people, and the right technology can unlock their potential at scale.

We saw a fragmented marketplace where skilled professionals struggled to reach clients, and businesses lost time trying to find reliable service providers. Our answer was a platform that doesn't just connect—it empowers.

Registered as a Limited Liability Company in Nigeria, Nkiri Synergies is committed to building the infrastructure that powers Africa's future of work.

How It Works

Simple. Seamless. Powerful.

Getting started with Nkiri Synergies takes minutes. Follow these simple steps to connect, collaborate, and transact.

01

Create Your Account

Sign up on the platform or download the Vast App. Complete your profile as a Service Seeker in just a few minutes — no technical knowledge required.

02

Post Your Service Request

Describe what you need, when you need it, your location, and your budget. Our platform will instantly show you matching, verified providers.

03

Get Matched with Verified Providers

Browse provider profiles, compare ratings and reviews, check availability, and select the professional that best meets your needs.

04

Communicate, Manage & Pay Securely

Chat directly with your chosen provider, track project progress on your dashboard, and process payments securely through Paystack or Flutterwave — all within the app.

05

Rate, Review & Repeat

After service completion, leave a review to help the community. Your feedback drives quality improvement and helps other users make informed decisions.

01

Apply & Get Verified

Sign up as a Service Provider on the platform or Vast App. Submit your credentials, complete a background check, and provide proof of liability insurance.

02

Build Your Profile & List Services

Create a compelling profile showcasing your skills, experience, portfolio, and pricing. The more complete your profile, the more likely clients are to choose you.

03

Receive Client Matches & Requests

Our algorithm matches you with clients who need your specific skills and services. Accept requests, review briefs, and communicate directly with potential clients.

04

Deliver, Collaborate & Get Paid

Use the project management tools in Vast App to deliver exceptional work. Track milestones, communicate updates, and receive secure, timely payments directly to your account.

05

Grow Your Reputation & Earnings

Collect positive reviews, build your reputation score, and unlock higher-value opportunities. Top-rated providers gain premium placement and access to exclusive corporate clients.
